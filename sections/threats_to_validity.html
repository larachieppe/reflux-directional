Threats to validity

Everything below is a reason to disbelieve a number in this report. The section is ordered from the threat that is structural and unfixable within a simulation, to the ones that are arithmetic and fixable in an afternoon. Two of them — the prevalence model and the base rate — change the interpretation of headline figures that are otherwise correctly computed.

1. Inverse crime: weaker here than usual, but not absent

Data are generated and analysed inside one forward model. In model-based EIT inversion this is the classic inverse crime: the reconstruction operator is built from the same discretised operator K that produced the data, so discretisation error cancels and reported image quality is unattainable on real measurements. That specific mechanism does not operate here, because the estimator never touches the forward operator. It consumes only the per-zone series and fits

(1)   tj = a + b zj  over zones j,  decision = sign(b + lag),  gated on wave linearity ≥ LIN_GATE

where zj is the axial centroid of the tetrapolar zone and tj its arrival time. Nothing in direction_features, xcorr_lag or decide_direction references the mesh, the CEM coupling blocks or the admittivity field. So the estimator inherits none of the FEM's discretisation error, which is exactly why the mesh study found the decision invariant across all five meshes while |Z| itself did not converge.

The residual bias is different and it is the serious one: the estimator's assumed response shape is guaranteed to match the simulated response shape. Equation (1) presumes each zone sees a single-peaked perturbation whose time of maximum tracks the bolus centroid past that zone. bolus_path produces precisely that, and says so — its docstring states that the bolus is a slug of essentially constant volume that moves, and that a rise/fall envelope was deliberately not imposed because an envelope makes the response separable as (amplitude per zone) × (one shared time course), and a separable response has no meaningful relative lag. That is a correct statement of physics and simultaneously an admission: the one modelling choice that would break the estimator was excluded from the simulator by design. Real ureteric peristalsis is a bolus that forms, propagates and is squeezed, i.e. partly separable. The fraction of a real per-zone signal that is separable envelope rather than travelling centroid is the unmeasured quantity in this project, and no run in the repository bounds it.

Reciprocity at 4.77e−15 and slope convergence to 0.11% are internal-consistency checks. Neither is evidence against inverse crime: both would pass identically if the simulated response shape were physically wrong, because both compare the model to itself. The test that would bear on it is external — a flow phantom with a peristaltic pump, where the per-zone waveform is measured rather than assumed, and the separable fraction can be estimated by fitting a rank-1 (amplitude × envelope) model and reporting the residual.

2. Model error the forward solver cannot see

  • Geometry. A right circular cylinder, R = 5.5 cm, height 20 cm, 6 rings, 17 layers. No spine, no psoas, no iliac crest, no ribs, no lordosis. The flank strips sit on a surface of constant curvature; a real flank is not one.
  • No bowel gas. TISSUE contains muscle, fat, kidney, bladder wall and urine. There is no air entry and base_sigma assigns none. Gas is a near-zero-conductivity inclusion that moves on its own schedule in the anterior and medial abdomen. It is the most obvious source of a spurious travelling perturbation with no relation to the ureter, and it is entirely absent. Study 6 tested body motion, which is a different confounder: motion displaces the whole conductivity field coherently, gas changes its topology locally.
  • Adiposity. Fat is present, as a shell at r > 0.82 R — a thickness of 0.18 × 5.5 = 0.99 cm in every simulated child, with no variation anywhere in the codebase. Since the strips are surface-mounted and the sensitivity of a tetrapolar pair falls steeply with depth, subcutaneous thickness is plausibly the single largest amplitude modulator in a real cohort, and it is held fixed.
  • Tissue values. Literature conductivities with a two-parameter dispersion, σ(f) = σ0(1 + 0.04 log10(f/50 kHz)) and εr(f) = εr0(50 kHz/f)0.10. This is a fit, not a Cole–Cole model, and pediatric tissue is not adult tissue. It matters more than usual because ωε/σ = 1.40 for kidney at 100 kHz: the permittivity term is load-bearing, so an error in εr0 is an error in the signal, not a rounding detail.
  • Urine conductivity is a constant. TISSUE["urine"] = 1.70 S/m, identical in every trial. Real urine spans roughly 0.5–3 S/m with hydration and osmolality, and a child undergoing a voiding study is deliberately hydrated, i.e. at the dilute end. The bolus signal scales with the urine–muscle contrast, 1.70 − 0.34 = 1.36 S/m as modelled; at 0.8 S/m urine the contrast falls to 0.46 S/m, a factor of ~3 in perturbation amplitude. Study 6 showed the failure mode is amplitude-driven and safe (abstention rises), so the expected consequence is a higher abstain rate rather than a wrong sign — but that is an inference from a different experiment, not a measurement. A one-line sweep of TISSUE["urine"] over 0.6–2.5 S/m would settle it and has not been run.

3. What the word "cohort" can and cannot mean here

This is the item most likely to be over-read by a reader skimming for results. draw_anatomy(world, rng) returns exactly six quantities, and inspection of them is decisive:

KeyWhat it drawsVaries?
ur_xureter lateral offset × U(0.92, 1.08)position
k_off, z_kidneykidney centre, N(0, 0.04R) and N(0, 0.03H)position
bladderbladder centre, N(0, 0.05R) / N(0, 0.03H)position
k_r(0.20R, 0.26R, 0.11H)fixed
b_r[0.30R, 0.30R, 0.12H]fixed

Every organ size is a fixed multiple of R and H, and R = 5.5 cm and H = 20 cm are constants of the run. xscale, the one knob that could make the torso elliptical, defaults to 1.0 and is never set otherwise. So: torso radius, torso height, torso cross-section, fat thickness, kidney size, bladder size and every tissue conductivity including urine are identical across all 48 "simulated children."

The 48 children are 48 draws of organ position inside one fixed body. That is a legitimate and useful ensemble — positional uncertainty is exactly what a placement study needs to marginalise over — but it means "cohort" here denotes a positional-jitter ensemble and nothing else. No result in this repository supports any statement about body habitus, age range, BMI, or scaling from infant to adolescent. In particular the Study 5 recommendation mixes parameterisations: the span is absolute (10.0 cm) while the centre is fractional (z_center = 0.34). On a torso half as long, a 10 cm strip covers twice the fractional height, and nothing here tests which of the two conventions transfers. The honest version of the recommendation is "10 cm at z = 0.34 on a 20 cm torso"; whether it generalises is an open question requiring a torso-size sweep that the code already supports (R, height and xscale are constructor arguments) and that has not been run.

4. Thresholds selected on the data they are reported on

There is no train/test split anywhere in this project. Two selections are made on the evaluation set:

  • LIN_GATE = 0.35 is a single hand-set module-level constant. Its docstring records a Youden justification from measured lin distributions at 0.45 cm motion (events median ~0.95, empty windows ~0.12–0.25; keeps 96.4% of events, rejects 71.4% of empties), which is a real calibration and a large improvement on the earlier hand-picked 0.55. But it is never swept anywhere in the repository, so no sensitivity of any reported figure to this constant exists. Every abstain rate quoted — 5.6% at 1 cm motion, 30.6% at 2–3 cm — is a function of one untuned number.
  • best_k in subject_analysis is chosen as argmaxk of Youden's J over the six candidate rules (flag if ≥ k of K = 6 events read retrograde), and best_sens / best_spec are then reported at that k, on the same 24 refluxing and 24 healthy children that chose it. With six candidates and n = 24 per arm, the winner's J is upward-biased by selection. Evidence that this is not hypothetical: the chosen k moves across cells — 3, 3, 4, 4 for the 10 cm arm at σ = 0/0.5/1/2 cm, and 1, 1, 1, 1 for the 16 cm arm — which is a threshold tracking noise as much as signal. The fix is standard: pre-register a rule (a simple majority, k ≥ 4) or select k on a disjoint set of children and report on the rest.

5. A prevalence model that is published but never called

GRADE_WEIGHTS = {1: 0.30, 2: 0.30, 3: 0.22, 4: 0.13, 5: 0.05} is defined at directional_sim.py:51 and rendered into the methods table by build_methods.py:76 as the project's grade model. sample_grade(rng) is defined at directional_sim.py:62 and, by exhaustive grep, called from nowhere. What run_precision.py actually does is

grade = int(np.random.default_rng(870_000 + c).choice([1, 2, 3, 4, 5]))

— uniform. So the realised mix is 40% grade IV–V against the declared 18%, and 40% grade I–II against a declared 60%.

Grade is not cosmetic in this model. GRADE_TABLE maps grade to (bolus semi-axis fraction of R, retrograde reach fraction of the ureter): 0.10 R and 0.20 at grade I, 0.30 R and 1.00 at grade V. Semi-axis 0.10 R → 0.30 R is a 27-fold volume ratio, and reach 0.20 → 1.00 determines how many zone centroids the bolus crosses, hence how many points equation (1) has to fit. Both are monotone in grade and both make detection easier.

Which figures this biases, and how. Optimistically, all of Study 5's detection figures: per_event_sens (0.806 / 0.743 / 0.681 / 0.632 across σ for the 10 cm arm), best_sens, and never_detected (0/0/4.2/8.3%). Under the declared weights the same per-grade rates would give a lower sensitivity and a higher never-detected rate, because the easy tail is over-represented by 22 percentage points. Less obviously, specificity is affected too: bolus_path uses GRADE_TABLE[grade] to size the bolus for antegrade trials as well as refluxing ones, so healthy children also get an unrealistically large mean bolus, giving cleaner true-negative calls — so best_spec (0.833 / 0.750 / 0.792 / 0.750) is optimistic as well. low_grade_never is the exception: it conditions on grade ≤ 2 and is therefore unbiased by the mix, which is fortunate, since it is the metric the placement conclusion rests on.

The re-weighted sensitivity cannot be recovered post hoc. metrics_precision.json stores only the aggregated grid, and the per-record checkpoint metrics_precision.partial.jsonl was not retained, so the per-grade breakdown no longer exists on disk. Correcting this needs a rerun with sample_grade actually called (or per-record grades persisted and the average re-weighted), not a recomputation.

6. The base rate problem

Every headline figure in this project is computed on a cohort that is 24 refluxing and 24 healthy children — 50% prevalence, a design choice, not epidemiology. Screening performance is governed by

(2)   PPV = (Se π) / (Se π + (1 − Sp)(1 − π))

Take the most operationally realistic Study 5 cell — 10 cm at z = 0.34 with σ = 1 cm of placement error, k ≥ 4 of 6 — where Se = 0.708 and Sp = 0.792:

Prevalence πSettingPPVNPV
50%this simulation's own cohort77.3%73.1%
30%after a first febrile UTI59.3%86.4%
1%general pediatric screening3.3%99.6%

Concretely, per 1000 children screened at π = 1%: 10 refluxers, of whom 7 are flagged and 3 missed; 990 healthy, of whom 206 are flagged. 213 children go on to confirmatory VCUG to find 7 cases, i.e.

(3)   VCUGs per true case = (Se π + (1 − Sp)(1 − π)) / (Se π) = 30.1

In a project whose entire clinical premise is avoiding catheterisation and radiation, a general-population screening deployment would order thirty catheterised studies per case found. At π = 30% the same device orders 358 VCUGs per 1000 instead of 1000, a 64% reduction, at the cost of missing 88 of 300 refluxers — a trade that is at least arguable against a reference standard that itself misses ~20% of reflux and whose graders agree only 59% of the time. The post-febrile-UTI population is the only setting these numbers support.

The corollary a device engineer should take from (2): specificity, not sensitivity, is the binding constraint. Holding Se = 0.708, reaching even a 30% PPV at π = 1% requires Sp = 98.3%, against the 79.2% measured. That is a 26-fold reduction in false-positive rate, not an increment. And the unexplained 11.1% false-retrograde floor at zero motion in Study 6 sits directly on this constraint: it caps specificity from below by a mechanism nobody in this project has yet identified.

7. Small cells, wide intervals, and a shape that should not be read

Study 5 is 2304 trials but only 48 children per cell, 24 per arm, and the low-grade subset is 14 children. Wilson 95% intervals on the numbers the placement conclusion rests on:

Quantity (cell)PointCount95% CI
Sensitivity, 10 cm, σ=170.8%17/2450.8–85.1%
Specificity, 10 cm, σ=179.2%19/2459.5–90.8%
Low-grade never-detected, 10 cm, σ=00%0/140–21.5%
Low-grade never-detected, 16 cm, σ=042.9%6/1421.4–67.4%

The two low-grade intervals abut at 21.4/21.5%: on any single cell the difference is at the very edge of conventional significance. What carries the finding is that the gap persists in all four σ cells and in both metrics. That is not four independent replications, however. The seeds make it a paired design: rng(870_000 + c) fixes each child's grade and c % 2 fixes refluxer status, both independent of span and σ, and rng(880_000 + child).normal(0, sigma) draws the misplacement from the same standard normal scaled by σ. So child c has the same grade, the same diagnosis and the same normalised misplacement in every cell of the grid. Pairing is a genuine variance reduction for the 10-vs-16 cm contrast — it removes between-child grade variance from the comparison — but it also means the four σ cells share their entire child-level randomness, so the non-monotonicity of the 16 cm arm (25 → 33 → 12 → 17% never-detected) is one realisation of the same 24 children re-scored, not four independent measurements of a trend.

Accordingly the defensible claim is the persistent gap between arms, not the shape of either curve in σ, and certainly not any interpolation such as "there is an optimum near σ = 1 cm." Resolving the shape needs independent child draws per σ and n on the order of 200 per cell; at the current per-trial cost that is a scheduling decision, not a research problem.

Standing limitations that no run addresses

The SNR budget is assumed, not measured from hardware. The tissue values are from the literature. The geometry is a cylinder. And there is no phantom data and no patient data of any kind — every number in this report is a property of a program.

The sentence to take away: this work establishes that a directional estimator is internally consistent, mesh-independent in its decision, robust to non-rigid motion in a fail-safe way, and sensitive to strip placement in a way that drives the choice of geometry — and it establishes none of that about a child, because the "cohort" is one body with jittered organs, the grade mix is 22 points easier than declared, and at realistic screening prevalence the measured operating point yields a 3.3% positive predictive value.
