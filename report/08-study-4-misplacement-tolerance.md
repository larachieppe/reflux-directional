> [!NOTE]
> **Tolerance is not symmetric, so quote it as a range and not a &plusmn;.** Moving the strip
> toward the bladder costs far less than moving it away: at +1 cm grades II&ndash;V still read
> 94&ndash;100%, while at &minus;1 cm grade V falls to 50%. The asymmetry is geometric &mdash; the
> ureterovesical junction sits low, so sliding down keeps the zones over the travelled segment
> while sliding up walks them off it.
