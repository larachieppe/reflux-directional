> [!KEY]
> **Low-grade reflux is a placement problem, not a limit of the method.** Grade II detection
> runs from 7% to 100% across the placements tested, on identical physics and an identical
> estimator &mdash; the only thing that changed is where the strip sits. A **shorter strip
> placed correctly beats a longer strip placed poorly**, because what matters is not span but
> how much of the lower ureter the zones actually cover.
